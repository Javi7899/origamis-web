Who it is for

For kids and adults starting from zero, and for folders who want to reach harder bases, modular origami and tessellations. All you need is square paper.

  • 256 models checked fold by fold: crane, jumping frog, dragon, phoenix, masu box, ninja star…
  • 53 paper airplanes that really fly, with the science of why they fly and how to trim them.
  • Every fold animated in 3D: pause, drag to watch in slow motion or turn the model around.
  • A 24-month course: basic folds, bases, reverse folds, modular, tessellations and your own designs.
  • Ten minutes a day, with review of the models you know and a from-memory mode.

Origamis is a two-year origami course for iPhone and iPad, in English and Spanish. Every step folds itself in front of you as an animation, with classic diagram notation. The first month is free; the full course unlocks with a single payment.

Frequently asked questions

What paper do I need?

15 × 15 cm origami paper. If you have none, cut a square from printer paper. The planes and the hat use a whole A4 or Letter sheet, and the jumping frog a card.

I can't get a step

Pause the animation and drag the bar to watch it in slow motion. Go back a step and check your paper matches the picture. If a step is unclear, write to me and I'll look at it.

I changed iPhone and don't see my purchase

Go to Progress › Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. The purchase works with Family Sharing.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. The app has no accounts and no servers. If you delete the app, your progress is lost.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole course and every model are inside the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore.
